The first release of the xtune client, for Android, Linux and Windows.

- Connect with one tap: Auto picks the config that works best on your network and fails over across every kind
  the server runs, including AmneziaWG, SSH tunnels and the DNS tunnel.
- Check your configs from your own device and network, and share the results with the server.
- Manage your xtune server: users, links and QR codes, configs, logs and a console.

Full-featured profiles need xtune with the client endpoints on the server (`xtune self-update` once released);
older servers work with their Xray configs.

Install: Android, the `arm64-v8a` APK; Linux, unpack and run `./install.sh`; Windows, unzip and run `xtune.exe`.
Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.
